# Amusnet-Hackathon

Open `My project/Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` in Unity and enter Play mode. Click **Play** in the main menu to start the Sprint 3 run facing the cider stall in Village Center. For a direct scene preview, open `Sprint3Village.unity`.

**WASD** moves the butterfly. **Space / Left Shift** change height. **E** sends a gust toward a nearby prop. Fly into a prop to touch it directly. Press **Tab** and click a district to fly to its starting point with your score intact. **R** starts a fresh run in the current district. In the main menu flow, **Esc** pauses the run and landing under large flower shelters helps you lose the predator.

## Repeatable combo tests

Open `My project/Assets/Scenes/Sprint3ComboTests.unity` and press **Play**. Choose a setup on the left, then press **E** or click **Gust**. **R** or **Reseed** restores the props, butterfly position, score, and gust cooldown, including during a chain. **Q** rotates the overview. The footer shows the actual hits and whether they came from the intended starter.

The blue and red springboard setups begin on opposite sides of the same board. The cask rebound needs a second gust while the barrel rolls. To rebuild the fixtures after editing the village, use **Tools → Sprint 3 → Regenerate Combo Test Scene** in Edit mode.

## The seven combos

| District | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| Village Center | Gust the striped awning. Apples scatter toward a hen and a cask. Gust the cask while it rolls to rebound through the bells and churns. Opening the gate changes the first impact. |
| West Hamlet | Hit the flying shuttle and follow the pink yarn. The second reel takes up the slack, pulls down the washing, and flushes a hen. |
| East Quarter | Approach the blue side of the fairground seesaw to launch a sandbag onto a second springboard and ring the bells. From the red side it lands in the churns beside the pottery instead. |
| South Bank | Lift the sluice, follow the water to the paddle wheel, and watch the outlet knock over the churns. |
| Flower Field | Gust the red poppy vane. Its seed heads fly to the cornflower and buttercup, splitting the reaction between the washing and the bell frame. |
| Pine Forest | Tip the first upright stack. The forked sawhorse strikes two more stacks, sending one into the bells and the other into the farm gate. |
| Orchard | Shake the laden bough. The fourth apple tips the picking basket into the cider press. The screw drives the platen down and the cork flies into the bell. |

Gust a fresh prop within 2.45 seconds of a nearby impact to extend the same chain. The district map tracks each area's best. An eight-hit chain earns a market stunt. Villagers investigate, chase, and briefly interrupt your gust if they catch you flying low. Climb or break line of sight to escape.

The village offers **319 mess points across 72 props**. Reach **280** to finish the timed run. Each prop scores once. First-time pacing still needs player feedback.

`Sprint2Workspace.unity` is the smaller flight test. `Sprint3ModelReview.unity` contains the original character and prop studies.
